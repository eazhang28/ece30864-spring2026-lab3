(a)
  Pros of Agile development:
    - Agile development is more flexible to work around because sprints enable pivoting.
    - Able to deliver software more frequently.
  Cons of Agile development:
    - A lot harder to project the long-term outcomes of projects because it is so flexible.
    - Documentation can be less thorough due to the shorter sprints and time to mature.

(b)
  Scrum Master roles and responsibilities:
    The Scrum Master facilitates scrum, which is intended to be done in lean and flexible sprints of work, which means that they have to hold meetings, meet with the team members one on one, enabling communication in the team, and projecting long term team wellness and productivity.

  Product Owner roles and responsibilities:
    The Product Owner is responsible for maximizing the value of the product by defining the vision of the product, and managing the backlog of tasks to handle to develop the product, as well as communicating between the development team and the stakeholders.

(c)
  Types of meetings in a sprint cycle:
    Sprint Planning:
      - take inventory of product backlog, reorganzie any priorities.
      - assign the items from the backlog to work on during the sprint.
    Daily Standup:
      - Track progress on the items in the sprint.
      - Identify obstacles currently existing in the sprint.
    Sprint Review:
      - Work with stakeholders to get feedback.
      - See what went right, or wrong, and adjust the team dynamics or work velocity as needed.

(d)
  Epic:
    Epics represent the high level ideas that need to be implemented in a project, and typically occur over multiple sprints.
  Story:
    Stories are a smaller unit of the Epic, typically done in a sprint, that describe the high level description of what needs to be done to support that epic goal.
  Bug:
    Bugs are reactive tasks that occur when a issue occures within any of the other work types, which are tracked to understand what could block other work.
  Task:
    Tasks are different from stories in that they are specific and about the impelmentation details involved in completing a story.
